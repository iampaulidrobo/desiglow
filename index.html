Questions

Do I need paid Lightroom?

No. The presets work in the free Lightroom mobile app on Android and iPhone. Desktop Lightroom and Camera Raw use the XMP files.

Will it look exactly like the samples?

Every photo has different light. Apply the preset, then adjust Exposure and Temp a little until the skin looks right. Newer Lightroom versions also have an Amount slider.

Do the video LUTs work in CapCut on my phone?

Use VN on your phone (Filter › import .cube). CapCut supports custom LUTs on PC and Mac.
